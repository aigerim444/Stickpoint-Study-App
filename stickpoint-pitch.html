Core Features — Study Methods
9 science-backed methods. All AI-coached.

Every method is powered by Claude AI — generating content, grading responses, and giving feedback in real time. Each one targets a different mechanism of memory consolidation.

1
Practice Testing AI GRADED
AI generates a full practice exam from your notes — short answer, multiple choice, and true/false. Answers are graded by AI with abbreviation and equivalent-phrasing recognition. Wrong answers get explanations. If Chop marks you wrong unfairly, an "I am correct" override button lets you dispute it and update your score.
2
Feynman Technique AI GRADED
Explain the concept as if teaching a 12-year-old. Chop checks whether you actually understood it — not just whether you wrote something. A strict scoring rubric rejects off-topic answers and confident-sounding nonsense, forcing real understanding.
3
Elaborative Interrogation AI GRADED
For each fact in your notes, Chop generates a why/how question and a connection question — both strictly grounded in your actual notes. You answer, then get an ideal model answer and a visual reasoning chain showing cause → mechanism → effect.
4
Problem Sets MATH AI GRADED
Detects math automatically and generates worked examples broken into step-by-step solutions. After studying the worked example, the student practices similar problems. Chop grades the work and identifies error types so weak areas are clear.
5
Concrete Examples
For abstract concepts, students generate a real-world example and Chop evaluates whether it actually demonstrates the concept — not just whether it mentions the right words. Reinforces application over recognition.
6
Blurting
Classic brain-dump technique: Chop presents a topic, student writes everything they know from memory. Chop scores recall percentage and highlights what was missed. Students can see exactly which topics are weak.
7
Self-Explanation
Notes are split into digestible chunks. After reading each chunk, students explain it in their own words. Chop identifies gaps between what they wrote and what the passage actually said.
8
Active Recall (Flashcards)
AI-generated flashcard deck with hard/medium/easy self-rating. Cards you find hard resurface more frequently. Built into the app — no third-party flashcard platform needed.
9
Pomodoro Focus Timer
AI chunks the notes into focused reading segments timed to Pomodoro intervals. Includes focus-quality ratings and "what was hardest?" reflection prompts after each session.

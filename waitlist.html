How it works
Three steps to studying smarter
1
Take the 2-minute diagnostic
A short quiz figures out where you get stuck when you study — not just what subject, but how you learn best.
2
Get matched to a real study method
Active recall, practice testing, the Feynman technique, spaced repetition — Stickpoint picks the one backed by research that fits how you actually learn.
3
Study your own material, coached
Upload your notes and Stickpoint generates flashcards, practice tests, and graded explanations — then drills what you keep missing until it sticks.
Backed by research
This isn't just a high schooler problem
A survey of college students found that most default to rereading their notes and textbooks when studying on their own, and few spontaneously test themselves on the material — even though retrieval practice is one of the most effective ways to actually learn something.
— Karpicke, Butler & Roediger, "Metacognitive strategies in student learning," Memory, 2009
A landmark review of study techniques found that the two strategies students reach for most — rereading and highlighting — are among the least effective for long-term learning, while techniques like practice testing and spaced practice work far better and are rarely taught.
— Dunlosky et al., "Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques," Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 2013

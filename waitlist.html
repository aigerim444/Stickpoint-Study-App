How it works

Three steps to studying smarter

1

Take the two-minute diagnostic

A short quiz finds where you get stuck when you study, not just which subject, but how you actually learn.

2

Get matched to a real study method

Active recall, practice testing, the Feynman technique, spaced repetition. Stickpoint picks the one backed by research that fits how you learn.

3

Study your own material, coached

Upload your notes and Stickpoint generates flashcards, practice tests, and graded explanations, then drills what you keep missing until it sticks.

Backed by research

You are not the only one guessing

"

A survey of college students found that most default to rereading their notes and textbooks when studying alone, and few spontaneously test themselves on the material, even though retrieval practice is one of the most effective ways to actually learn something.

Karpicke, J. D., Butler, A. C., and Roediger, H. L. "Metacognitive strategies in student learning: do students practise retrieval when they study on their own?" Memory, 2009.

"

A wider review of study techniques reached a similar conclusion: the two strategies students reach for most, rereading and highlighting, are among the least effective for long-term learning, while techniques like practice testing and spaced practice work far better and are rarely taught.

Dunlosky, J., Rawson, K. A., Marsh, E. J., Nathan, M. J., and Willingham, D. T. "Improving students' learning with effective learning techniques." Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 2013.
